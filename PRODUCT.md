# ULURA Coffee House — PRODUCT

**Register:** brand (design IS the product — a marketing site for a café).

## What it is
A high-conversion marketing website for **ULURA Coffee House**, a specialty
coffee house on Petra Street, Irbid, Jordan. Two pages:
- **Landing** (`index.html`) — story + proof + a clear path to the menu and to visiting.
- **Menu** (`menu.html`) — the full, custom-designed menu (not a photo of the printed one).

## Audience & job
Locals and visitors in Irbid deciding where to get coffee / sit / order. The
site's job is to convert a curious visitor into a **visit, a call, or a
directions tap** — fast, on mobile, often late at night.

## Business facts (source of truth)
- Rating: **5.0 on Google (67 reviews)** · Price: **JOD 1–5**
- Address: **Petra St., Irbid, Jordan**

## Conversion priorities
1. Repeated, obvious CTAs: **View Menu**, **Get Directions**, **Call**.
2. Trust signals surfaced early: 5.0 ★, 67 reviews, open till 2 AM.
3. Mobile sticky action bar (Call · Directions · Menu).
4. Specialty differentiator: the 6-method **Manual Brew Station**.

## Voice
Warm, crafted, calm, confident. "Poured with intention." Not shouty, not
corporate. Specialty-coffee sincerity.
